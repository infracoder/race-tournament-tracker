📋 How It Works

1. Registration: Each of the 9 players registers with their name and receives a player letter (A-I).

2. Racing: Each player races exactly 4 times against different opponents across 9 total races.

3. Scoring: F1 points system - 1st place: 25pts, 2nd: 18pts, 3rd: 15pts, 4th: 12pts.

4. Tiebreakers: Most 1st places → Most 2nd places → Most 3rd places.

5. Winner: After all 9 races, the player with the most points wins! 🏆
